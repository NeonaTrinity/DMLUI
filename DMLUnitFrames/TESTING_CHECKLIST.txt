DMLUI Unit Frames 2.0.95 - test checklist

TARGET OF TARGET
- With Show target's target ON and Override Blizzard target of target frame OFF, Blizzard ToT remains visible and the DML ToT is not duplicated.
- With Show ON and Override ON, Blizzard ToT is hidden and the DML ToT is shown.
- With Show OFF, both stock and DML ToT are hidden regardless of Override.

AGGRO / COMBAT
- Enable Highlight unit frame aggro. Pull a mob on Player and Party members; the unit currently tanking/holding aggro gets a red frame border.
- Enable Display combat icon. Player and Party member frames show crossed swords while UnitAffectingCombat is true and hide them afterward.
- Pet / Party Pet / Target / Focus frames do not receive these Player/Party-specific indicators.

PARTY RANGE FADING
- Enable Fade party frames when out of range.
- Test 15 / 25 / 30 / 35 / 40 yd selections where practical for the current class.
- Fade-to numeric entry clamps to 10-90.
- Out-of-range party frames use the chosen resulting opacity and restore alpha 1 when back in range.
- Target an out-of-range party member; the DML Target frame uses the same party fade.
- Disabling party fading restores all Party member and relevant Target alpha to 1.

ENEMY TARGET RANGE FADING
- Enable Fade enemy target if out of range.
- Hostile Target fades to the configured opacity when its range checker reports out-of-range.
- Friendly/non-party targets are not affected by the enemy-target rule.
- Party targets continue to use the Party rule when Party fading is enabled.

RANGE SAFETY
- Verify no global item tooltips become stuck on Retrieving item information.
- Unit Frames range code must not query GetItemInfo / IsItemInRange.
- Learn/unlearn/change talents and verify no Lua errors from range-cache refreshes.

BASE / PARTY REGRESSION
- Player/Target/Focus/Pet replacements still enable independently.
- Left click targets; right click opens Blizzard-style unit menus.
- Frame scale remains clamped 0.50-2.00 and scales complete frames.
- Grouped Party anchor, spacing, Right/Below pet placement, and freeform per-unit + handles still work.
- Party Members and Party Pets retain shared scale categories.
- Reset Frame / Reset All Frames / Reset Settings still behave as before.

COMBAT SAFETY
- Protected enable/disable/layout changes remain blocked during combat with a chat message.
- Aggro border, combat icon, and alpha fading update during combat without moving/showing/hiding protected unit frames themselves.
